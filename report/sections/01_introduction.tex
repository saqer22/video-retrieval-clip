\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Text-to-video retrieval aims to rank a corpus of videos by their semantic relevance to a user's natural-language query.
The task is fundamental to multimedia search, video understanding, and content recommendation.

State-of-the-art approaches leverage vision-language pre-training, most prominently CLIP \cite{radford2021clip}, which learns a joint embedding space for images and text.
Our method extends CLIP to the video domain by sampling frames, encoding them independently, and aggregating into a single video embedding via mean pooling.

This report presents:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item A fully offline, CPU-friendly evaluation pipeline.
    \item Baselines (random, single-frame CLIP, multi-frame CLIP) compared against our proposed method.
    \item Metrics including Recall@K, MedR, MeanR, mAP, and retrieval latency.
\end{enumerate}
