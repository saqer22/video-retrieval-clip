\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Dataset}
For demonstration purposes we generate a small synthetic dataset of 20 short videos (2 seconds, 64$\times$64) depicting colored shapes moving in various directions. Each video has 3 captions. The split is 12/4/4 (train/val/test).

Our pipeline also supports MSVD \cite{chen2011msvd} and MSR-VTT subsets \cite{xu2016msrvtt} when locally available.

\subsection{Implementation Details}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Backbone}: OpenCLIP ViT-B/32 with OpenAI pre-trained weights.
    \item \textbf{Frames}: Extracted with OpenCV at a target size of 64$\times$64 (demo) or 224$\times$224.
    \item \textbf{Frame Count}: We test 1, 4, and 8 uniformly sampled frames.
    \item \textbf{Device}: CPU by default, auto-detect GPU.
    \item \textbf{Reproducibility}: Deterministic seed across NumPy, PyTorch.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Baselines}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Random}: Videos ranked randomly.
    \item \textbf{Single-frame CLIP}: Only the middle frame.
    \item \textbf{Multi-frame (4/8)}: Mean over 4 or 8 frames.
    \item \textbf{Proposed}: Multi-frame with optional projection head (head off in demo to stay fast).
\end{enumerate}
