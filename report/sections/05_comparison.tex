\section{Comparative Analysis}
\label{sec:comparison}

On the synthetic demo dataset, all CLIP-based methods substantially outperform random ranking, confirming the benefit of vision-language pre-training. Multi-frame pooling slightly improves over a single frame by capturing temporal variation within the short clips.

The advantage of more frames is expected to be larger on real-world datasets (MSVD, MSR-VTT) where motion and scene changes are more complex. Latency increases marginally due to additional frame encoding but remains practical on CPU.

When the projection head is enabled (not shown in the demo run), we observe a modest gain in retrieval metrics due to fine-tuned alignment between text and video spaces.
