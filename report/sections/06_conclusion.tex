\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented a portable, CPU-first text-to-video retrieval pipeline that:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Requires no external data downloads or GPU for a complete end-to-end demonstration.
    \item Leverages frozen CLIP encoders with mean pooling of sampled frames.
    \item Computes standard retrieval metrics (Recall@K, MedR, MeanR, mAP) and latency.
    \item Generates a complete LaTeX report with auto-filled tables.
\end{enumerate}

Future work could include training the optional projection head on larger datasets and exploring temporal attention mechanisms.
